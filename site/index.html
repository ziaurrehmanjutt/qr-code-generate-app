Features

DiyaQR is a fast, beautiful QR code creator and scanner. Make a QR code for a link, Wi-Fi, contact, event or location, style it your way, then save or share it in seconds. Scan any QR code or barcode with the camera or from a picture, and act on it in one tap.

✨

Create any QR code

  • Text, links, email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi, contact cards, calendar events and map locations
  • Live preview while you type
  • Use your current location for map codes
🎨

Make it yours

  • Ready-made color styles, plus linear and radial gradients
  • Choose the shape of the dots, the corner frames and the corner dots
  • Add your own logo from the gallery or an image link
  • Simple error-correction levels with clear explanations, and a warning when a design may be hard to scan
📷

Scan and act fast

  • Scan with the camera or pick a picture from the gallery
  • Smart actions: open a website, call, send an SMS or email, open in Maps, add to calendar, save a contact or connect to Wi-Fi
  • Safety hints for shortened links, plain http links and links that point to an IP address
💾

Save, share, edit

  • Export as PNG, JPEG, SVG or WebP
  • Save to your gallery, share to any app, or keep a code in the app and edit it later
  • Scan history, favorites and search
🌍

Made for everyone

  • 12 languages: English, العربية, Español, Français, हिन्दी, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia, Português, Türkçe, বাংলা, Filipino and فارسی, with full right-to-left support
  • Light, dark and system themes
